Who we are

Our heart and calling.

We are a Generation of the Spirit: a people committed to actualising the will of God by the help of the Holy Spirit.

“We love and celebrate you. You belong to a family that desires to see Jesus formed in every life.”

Our fellowship is centred on Jesus Christ, the living and enduring Word of God, prayer, purity, discipleship and a life that reflects Him.

01 / MISSION

Our Mission

To preach the Gospel of Jesus Christ to all creation and make disciples. Mark 16:15; Matthew 28:19–20.

02 / VISION

Our Vision

To present every man — male and female — approved before God, through the living and enduring Word of God.

“Jesus must be seen and Jesus must be heard.”

Our conviction
Life together

Our group rules.

To keep this family edifying, peaceful and centred on Christ, every member is asked to observe these guidelines.

Let love lead in all that we do.

Respect and love one another. Let love lead. John 13:34.
No posting of nude pictures, videos, stickers, or any immoral content.
No advertising, business adverts, investment links, or content contradicting the Gospel of Christ. Please use the Discussion Group in the community for advertisement.
Feel free to bless us with messages, prayers, scriptures and contents that will help our spiritual growth.
No arguments, insults, or quarrels. We are one in Christ.
Receive Jesus Christ as your Lord and Saviour. If you haven't, you can kindly reach out to us using the contact details below.
